Why Sriperumbudur?

Fast emerging neighbourhood of Chennai, strategically located on the Chennai-Bangalore Highway (NH4). It is approximately 34 km from downtown Chennai. It is part of the Kancheepuram District. It is Western part of the suburbs of the Chennai Metropolitan Area. Over the last decade, it has seen significant investments by large manufacturing industries and the Tamil Nadu Government. The State Industries Promotions Corporation of Tamil Nadu (SIPCOT) has set up a large industrial complex aimed primarily at the manufacturing industry as well as a special economic zone (SEZ) for the footwear industry.

Irungattukottai is the location of Hyundai Motors large manufacturing facility. The plant forms a critical part of Hyundai's global export hub. It currently exports to around 85 countries across Africa, Middle East, Latin America, Australia, and Asia Pacific..
